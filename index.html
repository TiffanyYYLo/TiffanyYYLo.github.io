About

I am a researcher working in probability theory. I am currently a postdoc at Stockholm University, supported by the Sverker Lerheden Foundation. My mentors are Maria Deijfen and Daniel Ahlberg. Before this I was a postdoc at Uppsala University with Cecilia Holmgren and Svante Janson, and I completed my PhD at the University of Melbourne under Nathan Ross.

Much of my work concerns fluctuations emerging from random structures such as evolving random graphs and percolation models, where the relevant quantities are often sums with long-range dependence. A tool that I frequently reach for is Stein's method. More broadly, I am also interested in graph limits and their interplay with structural properties.
